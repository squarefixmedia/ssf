Our Focus Areas

Empowering change for a brighter tomorrow

01 / 06

Health and Hygiene

Promoting awareness, preventive care, and community-based health initiatives.

We conduct menstrual hygiene awareness sessions, eye check-up camps, HIV, Cancer, and sickle cell awareness drives, and community health education programs in underserved areas to connect with Government Schemes.

02 / 06

Education Support

Breaking poverty cycles through remedial education programs and learning access.

We provide tuition classes, foundational literacy support, educational materials, and recreational engagement for children in slum communities.

03 / 06

Women Empowerment

Skill development and livelihood training for financial independence in urban and rural areas.

Through skill development programs such as cake-making and tailoring, we support women in building sustainable livelihood opportunities and connecting with Govt Schemes.

04 / 06

Disaster Relief

Rapid response and community recovery during crises.

We mobilise resources during floods, pandemics, and emergencies to provide direct assistance to affected families.

05 / 06

Disability Inclusion

Promoting awareness, dignity, and equal opportunities.

We promote awareness and inclusion initiatives, connect with Skill Training and Livelihood, refraction programs, and connect with Government Schemes.

06 / 06

Elderly Care

Supporting senior citizens with dignity and compassion.

We provide winter essentials and Medicinal help, a health camp for elderly people, Nutritional Support and conduct awareness programs addressing the needs of senior citizens connecting with Government Schemes.

Our Story

More about us

Stepping Stone Foundation is a registered society under the Society Registration Act (2016) and the Bombay Public Trust Act (2017). We operate with a mission to empower underprivileged communities, particularly women and children, through access to education, healthcare, livelihood training, and social support systems.

We are committed to advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and contributing to the nation’s overall development. As a grassroots organisation, we work closely with communities to create sustainable, inclusive, and lasting change, with a special focus on the well-being and development of the most vulnerable children and their families.

Our Inspiration

Our inspiration, the late Mrs. Medlin Gour, was a devoted nurse who dedicated her life to selfless social service. At a time when very few organizations were working in this space, she tirelessly served the poor, laborers, and the sick with compassion and commitment. Her unwavering efforts brought hope to many lives and sparked meaningful steps toward social progress. Without seeking recognition, she quietly laid the foundation for a culture of service, becoming a lasting inspiration for others to follow.

“

Service is not charity, it is a responsibility.

With a background in Social Work (MSW), our foundation was established with the belief that even small interventions can create meaningful transformation. We invite individuals, institutions, and corporates to join us in building resilient communities.

Mr. Moses Gour, Founder

Livelihood

Women’s Handmade Products

Our organisation conducts cake-making, candle-making, and tailoring classes to equip women with practical livelihood skills and promote their economic independence. Through these training sessions, participants learn to prepare attractive candles, stitch garments, and make useful products.

In addition, entrepreneurship development training is provided to help beneficiaries understand the basics of starting and managing a business. These initiatives have helped empower women by creating opportunities for self-employment, income generation, and a more sustainable livelihood.
